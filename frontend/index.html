📖 ZDownloader Complete Usage Guide

Hamesha fast aur smooth downloading ke liye ye aasan steps follow karein.

⚡
1. Web App Instant Downloader
  • 1 Kisi bhi video ka link copy karein (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat, Pinterest, etc.).
  • 2 Web App ke ⚡ Instant tab me link paste karke Analyze par click karein.
  • 3 4K, 1080p, 720p ya 320kbps MP3 par click karein ➔ download foran start ho jayega.
  • 4 ✂️ Lossless Trimmer use karke sirf specific video clip (e.g. 00:30 to 01:15) download karein.
📋
2. Bulk & YouTube Playlist
  • 1 📋 Bulk & Playlist tab par click karein.
  • 2 Ek saath 1 se 50 video links line-by-line paste karein YA YouTube Playlist link dalein.
  • 3 Analyze All Links par click karein ➔ saari videos list ho jayengi.
  • 4 Jo jo videos chahiye tick karein aur ⚡ Download Selected dabayein!
🧩
3. Chrome Extension & In-Page Buttons
  • 1 Chrome me kisi bhi video page par jayein (jaise Instagram Reel, Pinterest, etc.).
  • 2 Extension icon par click karein: active video automatically detect ho jayegi.
  • 3 Website par video ke upar floating ⚡ Download button par click karke bhi 1-click download kar sakte hain!
🖱️
4. Right-Click Instant Download
  • 1 Chrome me kisi bhi video link ya chalte hue video player par Right-Click karein.
  • 2 Menu me ⚡ Download with ZDownloader par click karein.
  • 3 Extension kholne ki bhi zaroorat nahi! Background me video automatically download ho jayegi.
💡
